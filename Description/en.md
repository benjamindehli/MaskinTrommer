MaskinTrommer is a drum instrument made entirely from analogue synthesizers. Kicks, snares, hi-hats, cymbals and toms were built on a Korg MS-20, a Roland SH-09, a Doepfer Dark Energy and a Sequential Circuits Pro-One, then sampled many times over so no two hits repeat exactly. The range runs from tight and percussive to big and sustaining.

Each drum section has its own mixer with one knob per synthesizer source, so a single kick can blend several instruments at once and each source contributes its own round robins. Kick and snare have a pitch control. Snare, hi-hat, cymbal and tom have a sustain control that chooses between short and long samples, or in Dynamic mode follows MIDI velocity, so how hard you play picks the length of the sound as well as its volume.

Globally there is a sensitivity control for how strongly velocity maps to level, an Impact control that balances the transient against the body of each hit, and a volume-compensated saturation stage. The on-screen keyboard is colour-coded to match the drum sections.

342 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored as lossless FLAC in one sample file that the installer puts in place.

## Features

- Kick, snare, closed and open hi-hat, cymbal and tom, all built on analogue synthesizers.
- Sources: Korg MS-20, Roland SH-09, Doepfer Dark Energy, Sequential Circuits Pro-One.
- Per-drum mixer: blend several synth sources on one drum, each contributing its own round robins.
- Deep round robins throughout, so repeated hits never sound identical.
- Pitch control on kick and snare and sustain-length control on snare, hi-hat, cymbal and tom.
- Dynamic mode, where MIDI velocity selects sample length as well as level.
- Impact control for the balance between transient and body.
- Volume-compensated saturation.
- Sensitivity control for how far velocity moves the volume.
- Colour-coded on-screen keyboard matching the drum sections.
- 342 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored losslessly (68 MB source library).
